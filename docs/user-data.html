How This Forecast Works

Each 5-minute slot is a weighted average of your logged fares. Rides logged closer to that time count more, and so do rides on the day you picked. Slots with no ride within about 25 minutes are left blank instead of guessed.

Cheap, average and pricey are split by thirds of your own fares. For the model trained on the full dataset, see the forecast page.

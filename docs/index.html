Where The Numbers Come From

Fares were recorded every 5 minutes over 8 weekday mornings, from 24 November to 3 December 2025, for 143 fares in total. They ranged from RM 21 to RM 70.

A random forest model was trained on 80% of them. On the other 20%, its predictions were off by RM 6.32 on average.

Rain made a big difference: rainy mornings averaged RM 52.46, clear ones RM 31.06. The forecast above assumes clear weather.
